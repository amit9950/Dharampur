About Dharmpur

Welcome to Our Village

Dharmpur is a vibrant village located in Etah District, Uttar Pradesh, with PIN code 207302. Positioned near Jalesar with excellent road connectivity, our village is a blend of traditional values and modern development.

Our village is blessed with a strong agricultural base, rich cultural heritage, and a compassionate community of over 795 voters. We take pride in our traditional crafts, local businesses, and the warm hospitality of our people.

With sustainable water resources including handpumps and a commitment to education and health, Dharmpur is continuously evolving while preserving its cultural roots.

Agriculture & Farming

Main Crops of Dharmpur

Dharmpur is primarily an agricultural village with fertile lands supporting sustainable farming practices. Our farmers cultivate the following major crops:

  • 🌾

    Wheat (गेहूँ)

    Primary winter crop, harvested in March-April

  • 🌱

    Bajra (बाजरा)

    Millet crop, drought resistant, harvested in September-October

  • 🍚

    Rice (धान)

    Monsoon crop, harvested in October-November

  • 🥔

    Potato (आलू)

    Winter vegetable crop, high yield and income source
